# AHRestock

Your hard-to-remember restock items one click away in the auction house.

AHRestock adds tabs to the auction house side bar with curated item lists for the
current expansion, so you no longer have to remember (or type) the names of all
the flasks, enchants and gems you need:

- **Consumables** – flasks, weapon oils, food, feasts, potions and more
- **Enchants** – gems and enchants for every slot
- **Crafting** – embellishments and missives

Clicking an item opens the auction house's buy view for it; you pick the quantity
and confirm the purchase as usual. A switch in the top-left corner chooses which
crafting quality (Q1 or Q2) is shown. Small badges on the icons mark the stat an
item gives (**C**rit, **H**aste, **M**astery, **V**ersatility, **P**rimary stat).

## Usage

- Open the auction house and click one of the AHRestock tabs in the side bar.
- `/restock [page]` (or `/ahr`) toggles a page – `consumables`, `enchants` or
  `crafting`, by name prefix or number. Outside the auction house the page opens
  as a free-floating window to look things up.

## Optional

[EllesmereUI](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/ellesmereui) is supported for
matching styling and fonts, but not required.
